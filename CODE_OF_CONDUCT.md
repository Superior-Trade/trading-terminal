# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the person you are replying to is trying to build something good.

## What we expect

- Give feedback on the code, not the person who wrote it.
- Accept that other people have context you do not, and ask before assuming.
- Take the disagreement seriously and the ego lightly.
- Remember that most contributors are doing this in their spare time.

## What we will not have

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, sexualised
language or imagery, publishing someone's private information, or sustained disruption
of discussions.

This project deals with money, and money makes people tense. That is a reason to be more
careful with each other, not less.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere else someone is
representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to **conduct@superior.trade**. Reports are handled privately and we
will not share who reported what.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that break this, and may temporarily or
permanently ban anyone whose behaviour warrants it. We will explain the decision to the
person it affects.
